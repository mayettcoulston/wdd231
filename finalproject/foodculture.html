Discover Nairobi's Culinary Heritage

Nairobi boasts a rich and diverse food culture influenced by traditional Kenyan cuisine and international flavors. From street food stalls to fine dining restaurants, Nairobi offers a wide range of culinary experiences that highlight local ingredients, traditional cooking methods, and modern twists.

Ugali

A staple maize meal served with vegetables, meat, or fish.

Nyama Choma

Grilled meat, often enjoyed with friends and family in a social gathering.

Sukuma Wiki

Collard greens sautéed with onions, tomatoes, and spices, usually served with ugali.

Mutura

Kenyan sausage made from minced meat, spices, and intestines, commonly barbecued.

Local Food Markets

Nairobi is home to vibrant food markets where locals sell fresh produce, meats, spices, and snacks. Visit Maasai Market, City Market, or Gikomba Market for an authentic taste of Nairobi's street food culture.
